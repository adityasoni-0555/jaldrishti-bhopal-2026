PROVISIONAL OPERATIONAL SCORING METHODOLOGY (2023–2025 HISTORICAL DATA)

Statutory limits are official Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) Class B outdoor bathing criteria (DO ≥ 5.0 mg/l, BOD ≤ 3.0 mg/l, pH 6.5–8.5). The Composite Risk Index (CRI), component weightings, and scaling constants (Peak Lead 1.5 mg/l ceiling, BOD excess 10 mg/l) are provisional operational planning tools developed for BMC officers and remain under review. Historical data (2023–2025) cannot guarantee present-day water safety on 20 October 2026. Real-time pre-immersion testing is recommended.

DRAFT FOR OFFICIAL REVIEW — prepared by JalDrishti; not an issued BMC order
OFFICE OF THE COMMISSIONER, BHOPAL MUNICIPAL CORPORATION (BMC)
MEMORANDUM | ENVIRONMENTAL SURVEILLANCE & VISARJAN MANAGEMENT
DATE: 11 October 2026 | EVENT: Durga Visarjan (20 October 2026)

SUBJECT: Water Quality Risk Hierarchy, Site Warnings & Deployment Directive across 7 Bhopal Immersion Ghats

1. Executive Summary:
Analysis of 1,524 water tests (2023–2025) monitored by MPPCB against Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) Class B standards reveals severe oxygen depletion (36.2% non-compliance; 4 complete anoxia readings) and organic overload (89.3% non-compliance). Immediate operational intervention is required to prevent fish kills, severe stench, and toxic paint dispersion.

2. Site Risk Ranking & Operational Hierarchy:
• Rank 1 — Bairagarh (Sant Hirdaram) kund (Upper Lake) [CRI: 69.58 | Tier 2]: Highest operational vulnerability. Experienced 4 complete anoxia events (DO = 0.0 mg/l) and peak Lead concentration of 1.770 mg/l. Mandate: Pre-position mobile aeration cascades 48h prior; divert large idols to lined tanks.
• Rank 2 — Malikhedi kund (Kund) [CRI: 51.05 | Tier 3]: Severe oxygen deficit (69.2% non-compliance, minimum DO 0.60 mg/l) and elevated organic loading (mean BOD 6.54 mg/l).
• Rank 3 — Khatlapura ghat (Lower Lake) [CRI: 46.39 | Tier 3]: Critical heavy metal accumulation from painted idols (Peak Copper 2.66 mg/l, Chromium 1.98 mg/l, Lead 1.27 mg/l).
• Rank 4 — Shahpura lake ghat (Shahpura Lake) [CRI: 46.33 | Tier 3]: Chronic organic decomposition (100% BOD non-compliance, average BOD 8.06 mg/l).
• Rank 5 — Rani Kamlapati / Kamla Park (Lower Lake) [CRI: 45.66 | Tier 3]: Elevated BOD (100% fail) and DO drop to 2.40 mg/l.
• Rank 6 — Hathaikheda dam / kund [CRI: 38.51 | Tier 4]: Well-oxygenated (DO min 5.10 mg/l) but vulnerable to acute post-immersion BOD surges up to 22.0 mg/l.
• Rank 7 — Prempura ghat (Upper Lake) [CRI: 28.92 | Tier 4]: Least degraded baseline; lowest mean BOD (3.56 mg/l).

3. Critical Data Gaps & Caveats:
The official MPPCB 2025 report omitted Durga immersion tables for Bairagarh and Malikhedi. This absence of recent records must not be construed as clean water. Real-time pre-immersion sampling is ordered on 18–19 October 2026.

1. कार्यकारी सारांश:
मध्य प्रदेश प्रदूषण नियंत्रण बोर्ड (MPPCB) के 2023 से 2025 के 1,524 जल परीक्षणों के केंद्रीय प्रदूषण नियंत्रण बोर्ड (CPCB) क्लास 'B' (स्नान जल) मानकों के आधार पर विश्लेषण से स्पष्ट है कि विसर्जन स्थलों पर गंभीर ऑक्सीजन संकट (36.2% उल्लंघन; 4 बार शून्य DO) तथा जैविक कचरा भार (89.3% उल्लंघन) विद्यमान है। जलीय जीवों के संरक्षण एवं प्रदूषण नियंत्रण हेतु तत्काल संसाधन नियोजन अनिवार्य है।

2. घाटवार जोखिम रैंकिंग एवं प्राथमिकता निर्देश:
• रैंक 1 — संत हिरदाराम (बैरागढ़) विसर्जन कुंड (बड़ा तालाब) [CRI: 69.58]: सर्वाधिक संवेदनशील स्थल। यहाँ घुलित ऑक्सीजन 4 बार शून्य (0.0 mg/l) दर्ज हुई तथा सीसा (Lead) 1.77 mg/l तक पहुंचा। अनिवार्य निर्देश: विसर्जन से 48 घंटे पूर्व कृत्रिम एरेटर (ऑक्सीजन संयंत्र) चालू करें एवं रासायनिक मूर्तियों को मुख्य जल से रोकें।
• रैंक 2 — मालीखेड़ी विसर्जन कुंड [CRI: 51.05]: 69.2% नमूनों में ऑक्सीजन की भारी कमी (न्यूनतम DO 0.60 mg/l) तथा उच्च बीओडी (औसत 6.54 mg/l)।
• रैंक 3 — खटलापुरा घाट (छोटा तालाब) [CRI: 46.39]: भारी धातुओं का अत्यधिक जमाव (तांबा 2.66 mg/l, क्रोमियम 1.98 mg/l, सीसा 1.27 mg/l)।
• रैंक 4 — शाहपुरा झील घाट [CRI: 46.33]: 100% नमूनों में बीओडी मानक का उल्लंघन (औसत 8.06 mg/l)।
• रैंक 5 — रानी कमलापति / कमला पार्क [CRI: 45.66]: 100% बीओडी उल्लंघन तथा ऑक्सीजन गिरकर 2.40 mg/l हुई।
• रैंक 6 — हथाईखेड़ा बांध / कुंड [CRI: 38.51]: ऑक्सीजन स्तर संतोषजनक (न्यूनतम 5.10 mg/l), किंतु विसर्जन पश्चात बीओडी 22.0 mg/l तक उछलता है।
• रैंक 7 — प्रेमपुरा घाट (बड़ा तालाब) [CRI: 28.92]: अपेक्षाकृत सबसे सुरक्षित स्थल; न्यूनतम औसत बीओडी (3.56 mg/l)।

          Regulatory Standards vs. Operational Scoring Methodology

          Technical documentation of statutory baselines, scoring formulas, and normalization ceilings

          Statutory · official

          1. Statutory Standards (Official Central Pollution Control Board)

          Under CPCB Class B (Outdoor Bathing Organised) criteria from cpcb_water_quality_criteria.csv:

          • Dissolved Oxygen (DO): Minimum ≥ 5.0 mg/l. Values < 5.0 mg/l cause aquatic hypoxia. 0.0 mg/l is total biological anoxia.
          • Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD): Maximum ≤ 3.0 mg/l (5 days at 20°C). Measures organic waste load consuming oxygen.
          • pH: Permissible range 6.5 to 8.5.
          Provisional · under review

          2. Provisional Operational Composite Risk Index (CRI)

          Because the CPCB does not publish a composite multi-parameter index, this model was designed for BMC decision support:

          CRI = (0.40 × S_DO) + (0.30 × S_BOD) + (0.20 × S_Metal) + (0.10 × S_Lagged)

          • S_DO (40% Weight): 0.60 × (DO Violation Rate %) + 0.40 × (Zero DO Anoxia Rate %)
          • S_BOD (30% Weight): 0.60 × (BOD Violation Rate %) + 0.40 × min(100, max(0, (Mean_BOD - 3.0)/10.0 × 100)). Excess ceiling of 10.0 mg/l represents BOD = 13.0 mg/l.
          • S_Metal (20% Weight): 0.70 × (Detection Rate %) + 0.30 × (min(1.0, Peak_Lead / 1.5) × 100). Lead ceiling of 1.5 mg/l represents a 15x exceedance of CPCB Schedule VI discharge limits (0.1 mg/l) and 150x exceedance of IS 10500 drinking limits (0.01 mg/l).
          • S_Lagged (10% Weight): Percentage of paired festival events where post-immersion 'after' conditions deteriorated further.
          Data quality

          3. Data Quality & Integrity Disclosures

          • BDL (Below Detection Limit): Preserved as non-elevated trace detection. Never converted to zero or counted as missing.
          • Missing Data: Excluded from compliance denominators and surfaced as explicit data gap alerts.
          • Durga 2025 Missing Data: Bairagarh and Malikhedi lack Durga 2025 tables in the MPPCB report.
          • Rani Kamlapati Site Ambiguity: Ganesh tables sampled an artificial kund; Durga tables sampled open lake water.
