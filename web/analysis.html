How the Composite Risk Index works CRI पद्धति

Statutory CPCB limits are kept separate from the provisional scoring choices made for this project.

Oxygen deficit 40% Organic load 30% Metal 20% Lagged 10%
Statutory · official

1. Statutory Standards (Official Central Pollution Control Board)

Under CPCB Class B (Outdoor Bathing Organised) criteria from cpcb_water_quality_criteria.csv:

  • Dissolved Oxygen (DO): Minimum ≥ 5.0 mg/l. Values < 5.0 mg/l cause aquatic hypoxia. 0.0 mg/l is total biological anoxia.
  • Biochemical Oxygen Demand (BOD): Maximum ≤ 3.0 mg/l (5 days at 20°C). Measures organic waste load consuming oxygen.
  • pH: Permissible range 6.5 to 8.5.
Provisional · under review

2. Provisional Operational Composite Risk Index (CRI)

Because the CPCB does not publish a composite multi-parameter index, this model was designed for BMC decision support:

CRI = (0.40 × S_DO) + (0.30 × S_BOD) + (0.20 × S_Metal) + (0.10 × S_Lagged)

  • S_DO (40% Weight): 0.60 × (DO Violation Rate %) + 0.40 × (Zero DO Anoxia Rate %)
  • S_BOD (30% Weight): 0.60 × (BOD Violation Rate %) + 0.40 × min(100, max(0, (Mean_BOD - 3.0)/10.0 × 100)). Excess ceiling of 10.0 mg/l represents BOD = 13.0 mg/l.
  • S_Metal (20% Weight): 0.70 × (Detection Rate %) + 0.30 × (min(1.0, Peak_Lead / 1.5) × 100). Lead ceiling of 1.5 mg/l represents a 15x exceedance of CPCB Schedule VI discharge limits (0.1 mg/l) and 150x exceedance of IS 10500 drinking limits (0.01 mg/l).
  • S_Lagged (10% Weight): Percentage of paired festival events where post-immersion 'after' conditions deteriorated further.
Data quality

3. Data Quality & Integrity Disclosures

  • BDL (Below Detection Limit): Preserved as non-elevated trace detection. Never converted to zero or counted as missing.
  • Missing Data: Excluded from compliance denominators and surfaced as explicit data gap alerts.
  • Durga 2025 Missing Data: Bairagarh and Malikhedi lack Durga 2025 tables in the MPPCB report.
  • Rani Kamlapati Site Ambiguity: Ganesh tables sampled an artificial kund; Durga tables sampled open lake water.

Data sources डेटा स्रोत

Monitoring evidence

  • MPPCB idol-immersion monitoring reports, 2023, 2024 and 2025 (Ganesh & Durga Visarjan), parsed to bhopal_ghats_2023_2025.csv. Each parameter row in a ghat's evidence panel cites the report year and PDF page.
  • CPCB Water Quality Criteria, Class B (Outdoor Bathing, Organised): cpcb_water_quality_criteria.csv.

What this tool is not

  • Not real-time monitoring. All readings are historical (2023–2025) and cannot establish current water safety.
  • Not an official BMC, MPPCB or CPCB publication or endorsement. The CRI is a provisional planning method under review.
  • MPPCB Ganesh and Durga Visarjan Report 2025 (PDF)
  • MPPCB Ganesh and Durga Visarjan Report 2024 (PDF)
  • MPPCB Idol Immersion Report 2023, CPCB format (PDF)
  • CPCB water quality criteria

Limitations सीमाएँ

  • Historical, not live. Readings are from 2023–2025 and cannot establish water safety today. Real-time pre-immersion testing is still required.
  • Missing records. The 2025 MPPCB report has no Durga tables for Bairagarh or Malikhedi; missing data is never treated as clean.
  • Limited parameters. No bacteriological parameters and only four heavy metals (Pb, Cr, Cu, Zn) were reported; nothing else is estimated.
  • Site ambiguity. Rani Kamlapati / Kamla Park: Ganesh tables sampled an artificial kund, Durga tables sampled open lake water (medium confidence).
  • Provisional index. CRI weights and ceilings are planning choices under review, not regulatory standards.
  • Year means are descriptive. Each year mixes festivals and phases, and some years have fewer readings (see n).
